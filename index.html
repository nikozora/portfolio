How I work

A simple process, so you always know what is happening.

Clear steps and plain updates, from the first call to the handover.

  1. Understand

    I ask what the business does, who the customers are and what the website must do.

  2. Plan

    I list the pages, fixes and tasks, and agree on them with you before I start.

  3. Build or fix

    I do the work in WordPress and Elementor and keep every page simple and light.

  4. Test

    I check phone, tablet and desktop, and send a test message through every form.

  5. Hand over

    I explain what changed, show you how to edit it and stay available for updates.
