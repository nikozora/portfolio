Auction company website

Ashman Company

WordPress Kadence woocommerce HTML/CSS

A website for an auction and appraisal company serving the Bay Area, California. The website features current, upcoming, and past auctions, along with dedicated service pages and an appointment booking option.

The challenge

The company needed a website that organized its auctions, showcased auction items, explained its services, and made it easier for visitors to book appointments.

My role

Worked on the website using WordPress and Kadence, with WooCommerce supporting the auction catalog. My responsibilities focused on the website elements and features I personally worked on.

The work I completed

Worked on website layouts to present the company's main page and business information. Worked on service pages covering selling, auctions, appraisals, and orderly liquidation, along with the appointment booking feature.

The outcome

The website brings auction listings, company services, and appointment booking together in one place, helping visitors find auction information and explore the company's services.
